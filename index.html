Sample Syllabus Topics & Important Questions

Computer Science Engineering:

  • Explain the phases of the Compiler Design process.
  • Differentiate between TCP and UDP protocols.
  • What is polymorphism in Object-Oriented Programming?

Electronics & Communication Engineering:

  • Draw and explain the working of a half-wave rectifier.
  • What is modulation? Explain different types.
  • Discuss the architecture of the 8051 microcontroller.

Mechanical Engineering:

  • Explain the laws of thermodynamics with real-life examples.
  • What is the difference between welding and brazing?
  • Explain gear trains and their applications.

Civil Engineering:

  • List and explain various types of foundations.
  • Discuss the properties of good building stones.
  • What are shear force and bending moment diagrams?

Electrical & Electronics Engineering:

  • What are different types of DC motors?
  • Explain the concept of power factor correction.
  • Describe the working of a transformer.

Information Technology:

  • Explain how the internet works using IP and DNS.
  • What is database normalization? Give types.
  • Discuss cybersecurity threats and countermeasures.
