Mechanical Engineering Subjects

Explore core subjects of Mechanical Engineering, covering thermodynamics, mechanics, materials, design, and production. Click on each subject to access notes and study resources.

  • Thermodynamics

    Study of energy, heat, work, and the laws governing energy transformations. Topics include the first and second laws, entropy, and thermodynamic cycles.

  • Mechanics of Materials

    Analyzes the behavior of solid materials under various forces. Learn about stress, strain, bending moments, shear force, and material strength.

  • Fluid Mechanics

    Focuses on the behavior of fluids (liquids and gases) in motion and at rest. Topics include Bernoulli's equation, laminar vs turbulent flow, and flow measurement.

  • Machine Design

    Application of engineering mechanics and materials to design mechanical components like gears, shafts, and bearings ensuring safety and efficiency.

  • Manufacturing Processes

    Covers casting, machining, welding, and forming processes. Learn about production systems and tools used in mechanical fabrication.

  • Heat Transfer

    Study of conduction, convection, and radiation modes of heat transfer. Applications in power plants, engines, and HVAC systems.
